## The challenge

Nova had a powerful analytics product but a marketing site that looked like a
weekend hack. Signups were flat, and the sales team was losing deals to
better-dressed competitors.

They came to us three weeks before a major product announcement.

## What we did

We ran a two-week discovery sprint to clarify positioning, then shipped:

- **New visual identity** - logo, type system, color palette
- **Marketing site** - Next.js, 12 pages, deployed on Vercel
- **Product screenshots** - motion-enhanced, story-driven
- **Sales deck template** - matching brand, editable in Figma

The entire project ran in a **four-week window** with weekly demos.

## Design decisions

The product is dense - data tables, filters, dashboards. We leaned into it
instead of hiding it. Real screenshots, real numbers, no stock illustration.

The homepage tells one story: **"You're drowning in data. Nova makes it obvious."**

## The result

- **+215%** signups in the first 30 days post-launch
- **4×** increase in demo requests
- Featured on Product Hunt - #2 product of the day
- Sales cycle shortened by ~20%

> "We went from being embarrassed by our website to sending it to investors.
> That's a night-and-day difference." - Marcus Lee, CTO

## Stack

Next.js · Tailwind · Framer Motion · Sanity (later migration) · Vercel
