## The challenge

Acme has been making precision parts since 1994. Their customers love them.
Their website repels new ones.

The brief: **"Make us look like the company we actually are - not the one our
website says we are."**

## Discovery

We spent two weeks on-site at their Ohio factory. Watched how parts were made,
talked to engineers, sat in on sales calls. What we found:

- Buyers said "trust" was the #1 factor - trust signals were absent
- Their best differentiator (in-house CNC tooling) was buried on page 4
- Sales team was emailing PDFs because the site couldn't handle spec sheets

## What we did

### Brand
- New wordmark and monogram - industrial, precise, timeless
- Photography direction: real factory, real people, natural light
- Tone of voice: plain, confident, technical

### Website
- 25-page rebuild in Next.js
- Live spec sheet generator (client-side PDF)
- Interactive factory tour
- RFQ form that routes by product category

### Sales enablement
- Pitch deck template
- One-pagers for top 20 SKUs
- Trade show booth refresh

## The result

- **+180%** inbound leads in 6 months
- **12 enterprise accounts** closed in Q1 post-launch
- Sales team stopped emailing PDFs - RFQ form does the work
- Referenced in two industry publications

> "We finally look like a company that can handle Boeing's order." -
> Tom Chen, VP Sales
