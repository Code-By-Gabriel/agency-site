## The challenge

Pulse had grown from 3 engineers to 30 in 18 months. Every team built its own
buttons, forms, and modals. The product looked like six different products.

Healthcare meant **WCAG AA** compliance wasn't optional — it was a legal
requirement.

## What we did

We built a two-part system:

### 1. Figma library
- Tokens (color, spacing, type, radius, shadow)
- 60+ components with variants
- Auto-layout everything, documented
- Light + dark + high-contrast themes

### 2. React component library
- Published as `@pulse/ui` on private npm
- Built on Radix primitives for accessibility
- Fully typed, tree-shakeable
- Storybook with usage examples

We ran three training sessions with their engineering team and shipped a
migration guide.

## Accessibility

Every component tested against:
- WCAG 2.2 AA (contrast, focus, keyboard nav)
- Screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA)
- 200% zoom, mobile, and reduced motion

## The result

- **~40% faster** feature delivery across teams
- **Zero** new accessibility bugs in the 6 months post-launch
- Designers and engineers now speak the same language
- Onboarded 8 new engineers in the time it used to take to onboard 2

> "It's the single highest-leverage thing we've done as a company." —
> Priya Shah, Head of Design
